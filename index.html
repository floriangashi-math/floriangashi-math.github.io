Descriptive Geometry Book

I wrote a book in Descriptive Geometry focused on geometric constructions, projection methods, perspective geometry, collinearity, and detailed solved exercises.

The aim of the book is to present Descriptive Geometry in a clear, rigorous, and visual way, especially for students who want to understand constructions step by step.

Title: Descriptive Geometry
Author: Florian Gashi
Status: In progress

The book includes topics such as:

  • Basic geometric constructions
  • Projection methods
  • Perspective geometry
  • Collinearity and incidence problems
  • Step-by-step solved construction exercises
