About

I am a Master's student in Mathematics at the University of Hamburg. I completed my Bachelor's degree in Mathematics at the University of Prishtina.

My mathematical interests are mainly in algebra, geometry, representation theory, tensor categories, topological field theory, and Hochschild cohomology.

I am especially interested in understanding mathematical structures through rigorous definitions, detailed proofs, examples, and visual explanations.

Descriptive Geometry Book Project

I am currently working on a book in Descriptive Geometry, focused on geometric constructions, projection methods, perspective geometry, collinearity, and detailed solved exercises.

The aim of the book is to present Descriptive Geometry in a clear, rigorous, and visual way, especially for students who want to understand constructions step by step.

Title: Descriptive Geometry
Author: Florian Gashi
Status: In progress

The book includes topics such as:

  • Basic geometric constructions
  • Projection methods
  • Perspective geometry
  • Collinearity and incidence problems
  • Step-by-step solved construction exercises
